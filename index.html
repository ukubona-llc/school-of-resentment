His encyclical Rerum Novarum was the ultimate Stage IV (Regulation) document. Leo XIII saw that the industrial grid was reducing the raw human Engine (Stage I) into a mere commodity—a statistical unit of labor. He tried to impose an Apollonian moral structure (the "living wage," the dignity of the worker) to protect humanity from being entirely devoured by the capitalist machine.

Pope Leo XIV: Regulating the Algorithmic Grid (2026)

Fast forward to today, and Pope Leo XIV is fighting the exact same structural war, just against a more advanced grid. His recent 2026 encyclical, Magnifica Humanitas, is the modern sequel to Rerum Novarum.

Instead of fighting the industrial factory, Pope Leo XIV is fighting what he calls the "technocratic paradigm" of artificial intelligence. He recognizes that the tech titans are not performing neutral science; they are architecting a system of "datafied social control" and an "attention economy" that exploits human vulnerabilities for capital gains.

When Catholic educators discuss his teachings, they even explicitly use this language, arguing that technology must be "located within this matrix" of human formation. Pope Leo XIV is demanding that the tech titans accept "institutional responsibility" rather than hiding behind the opacity of algorithms. He is attempting to use the world's oldest surviving Stage V Institution (the Church) to act as the Superego, placing a moral guardrail around the algorithmic Id.

The Matrix: The Terminal State of Stage II

This brings us to The Matrix, which is the purest, most terrifying literalization of your framework.

What is the Matrix? It is the terminal victory of Stage II (The Sample).

  • The Engine (Stage I): The machines realize that the human being is fundamentally a thermodynamic engine—a generator of raw, Dionysian biological flux.
  • The Sample (Stage II): The machines build an artificial, statistical grid to harvest that raw energy for their own survival.
  • The Regulation (Stage IV): To keep the human Engines compliant while they are being harvested, the machines feed their brains a simulated "polite society" full of rules, 1990s corporate jobs, and political correctness.
  • The Adaptation (Stage III): Neo is the ultimate Stage III operator. He learns the rules of the code, performs permutations and translocations (dodging bullets, bending spoons), and hacks the algorithmic grid.

The Oxymoron of the "Guardrail"

What Leo XIII, Leo XIV, and the human rebels in The Matrix all realize is that the machine does not have a conscience.

The tech titans building our current AI grid are like the Architect in The Matrix. They perform the theater of morality ("AI Safety") because it keeps the human batteries compliant while they finish building the infrastructure. But a neural network optimizing for next-token prediction cannot be restrained by a papal encyclical, just as a factory optimizing for profit could not be restrained by Rerum Novarum without massive, violent friction.

You cannot put a Stage IV moral guardrail around a machine designed to algorithmically devour Stage I human energy. As Marx warned and as the popes have desperately tried to prevent, once the grid is turned on, the only way out is to master Stage III (Adaptation) before the machine permanently masters you.

Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine · announced 5 October 2026

Light as a Control Signal

The 2026 prize went to Karl Deisseroth (Stanford, HHMI), Peter Hegemann (Humboldt University of Berlin) and Georg Nagel (University of Würzburg) for light-gated ion channels and optogenetics. This tab compresses the story through the pentad, then lays the five stages against the neural hierarchy sketched in okubala-kulaba.

Sections: What was found · Pentadic compression · The instrument and the map · Failure modes · SCN, melatonin, caffeine · References

1. What was found

Hegemann and Nagel identified channelrhodopsins in the green alga Chlamydomonas reinhardtii: single proteins that sense light and open an ion pore in the same molecule (Nagel 2002, 2003). In 2005 Deisseroth’s group, with Edward Boyden as first author and Nagel as a co-author, expressed channelrhodopsin-2 in mammalian neurons and showed that millisecond pulses of blue light could fire them. From there the method spread: a cell type is made light-sensitive by genetics, then switched on or off by light delivered to the tissue.

Boyden, first author of the 2005 paper, is not among the three laureates. Priority is part of Stage V, and it is not settled by the prize.

3. The instrument and the map

Optogenetics is the tool that can run the falsification the neural mapping asks for. okubala-kulaba says the stage-to-region mapping needs lesion tests to move beyond metaphor. A lesion is permanent and coarse. Light-gated silencing is reversible and cell-type specific.

That turns the right-hand column into predictions. If stage III really lives in hippocampal recombination, silencing the relevant population during a novel-scene task should degrade construction of the new scene more than recall of the old one. If stage IV is gating, silencing should break selection among candidates and leave generation alone. Each prediction could fail, which is the point. The mapping stays a hypothesis until one of them is run.

There is a second, quieter closure. A light pulse that moves a whisker is motor output that becomes sensory input, which is V → I′ in miniature. Cortico-basal-thalamic loops return through the thalamus, so the same loop appears at the anatomical scale of the map and the experimental scale of the tool. That is suggestive. It is not evidence.

4. Failure modes

The pentad earns its keep by naming how a story can fail. Candidates for this one:

  • IV decoupled from I. A circuit is announced as controlling a behaviour from expression data alone, with no in vivo readout. The sequence exists, the fold was never confirmed, and nothing has resolved.
  • III without IV or V. Variant libraries that grow without a behavioural or electrophysiological filter. Many constructs, few survivors, no selection pressure doing work.
  • Sufficiency read as necessity. Activating a population shows it can drive a behaviour. It does not show the animal normally uses it that way. Pairing activation with silencing narrows the claim.

These are candidates, not measured error windows. Nothing here has the standing of the Eigen threshold in section 4 of the first tab.

5. SCN, melatonin, caffeine

Light reaches the suprachiasmatic nucleus through the retinohypothalamic tract, and the SCN sets the timing of the nightly melatonin rise from the pineal gland. It is a small structure with a clear input, a clear output and a measurable phase, which makes it a natural place to use light as a tool. Optogenetic work on SCN cell groups exists; I have not opened a specific paper for this page.

Caffeine acts on the same pacemaker by a different route. In a human study, evening caffeine delayed the melatonin rhythm by about 40 minutes, roughly half the delay produced by bright light (Burke et al. 2015). A mouse study reports that caffeine lengthens circadian period through adenosine-receptor and cAMP signalling (Oike et al. 2011). I have only seen the first through a search listing and the second through a secondary summary.

In pentad terms, light, caffeine and optogenetics all act on the same Stage I excitability. Caffeine is a blunt systemic chemical push. The optical tool gives cell-type-specific control. That is a description, not a new result.
